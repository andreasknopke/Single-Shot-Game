The Dark. Your torch burns fuel. In darkness your Sanity erodes and the Choir grows bolder. Find light, keep moving.

The Loop. Each floor is new. Loot, level, and grow. Descend the stairs. On Floor V, the Choir waits. Kill it to win. Die, and the run ends.

Growth. Kills grant XP. Level up to choose a boon. Equip weapons, armor, and rings from the loot you carve from the dead.
